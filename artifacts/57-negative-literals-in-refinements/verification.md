## Lexer stand-in: findings

- Real lexer (`bs_lexer.xrl`): `-` is its own rule (`{'-', TokenLoc}`), integers are `{D}+` with no sign, `>=` and `<=` are their own tokens, `value` and `int` are `lident`, keywords are `{'where', Loc}`, whitespace is skipped (no layout tokens), and `TokenLoc` is `{Line,Col}` (`rebar.config` `error_location, column`). `lex_mini` emits the same shapes: `{'-',{L,C}}` then `{integer,{L,C},5}`, `{'>=',{L,C}}`, `{lident,{L,C},value}`, `{'where',{L,C}}`. So `-5` reaches `bs_parser` as `'-' integer` from both, and the parser then builds `e_neg` in both. The lexer cannot manufacture the `e_neg` shape.
- The only lexers' differences that could matter for the probed programs: none found. `lex_mini` emits `true/false` as keyword tokens where the real lexer emits `atom_lit`, and lacks `var/record/fn/...`; no probe uses them. Real leex takes the longest match; `lex_mini` orders `->` before `-` and `>=`/`<=` before `>`/`<`, which is equivalent for these inputs. Real `1..5` and float rules are not exercised except `-5.0`, where `lex_mini` uses a regex and produces `{float,_,5.0}` like the real rule.
- Mis-statement 1: the brief says the shim is "only used by `bs_check:pasted_signature/1`". `bs_check.erl:2248` (`declarations_pasted/3`) also calls `bs_lexer:string/1`. That path builds inexhaustive-diagnostic suggestions, so for P6b/P7a the suggestion text may come from `lex_mini`. The reported verdict and residual (`bs_types:to_pattern(Res)`) do not depend on it. Correct the sentence.
- Mis-statement 2: the P10 "no layout tokens" excuse (row 7).
- The hand-lexed probes do not run `bsc.erl`'s driver, so any pre-check pass in `bsc.erl` (742-776) or the diagnostic rendering is unexercised; the brief already says "nothing after the checker was run".
