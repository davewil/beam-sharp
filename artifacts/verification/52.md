## Circularity findings
- No fixture-edit or filtered-output circularity found. Each probe has a real failure path (erlc/elixirc/gleam could have warned and did or did not).
- Timing probe is **unfair as a comparison**: `code:which` on an absent module is the one slow path (full path scan on OTP 25 only), and it is compared against `lib_dir` of an absent app. The common case in Option A (module present) costs 5.1 us for `which(lists)`, the same as `lib_dir(stdlib)` at 5.3 us (my run). The probe prints `which(lists)` but the brief omits it. The "200x" is thus a property of OTP 25's missing-module scan, not of A versus B.
- module_to_app.escript hard-codes `code:add_pathz` for Elixir; absence of Req/epgsql is guaranteed by the machine, not discovered. That is the intended scenario, not a defect, but "maps to nothing" is a restatement of the setup.
- corpus_using.sh `cd ../../..` makes it location-dependent: from a copy it scanned `/` (116 hits). Repo location gives the brief's numbers. Its first line has a no-op `grep -v artifacts` on content lines.

## Corrections the brief needs
1. Replace "`code:which(missing_module)` = `non_existing` in **~430-520 µs** (it scans the whole code path). The application check is ~200x cheaper than the module check." with: "On OTP 25, `code:which(missing_module)` takes ~450-1350 µs (it scans the code path; varies with path length); on OTP 28 it takes ~8 µs. `code:lib_dir(missing_app)` takes ~2 µs on both. For a module that is present, `code:which` (~5 µs) and `code:lib_dir` (~5 µs) cost the same."
2. Replace "Evidence: derivation works for 11 of 14 modules" with "derivation yields an application for 10 of 14 modules (`erlang` is preloaded and has no application; `json`, `Elixir.Req`, `epgsql` are absent on OTP 25)."
3. Replace "recording costs 40 B" / "76 B for three" with "40 B for one, 68-76 B for three depending on OTP (OTP 28: 68 B)", and drop the 644 -> 684 figures (I measure 592 -> 632 on OTP 25, 580 -> 620 on OTP 28).
4. Add to "2 of 30": "(`Elixir.Req` appears only in the 51a prototype, `epgsql` only in exemplar 25d.)"
5. Remove "Behaviour on OTP 28 for the `code:` probes" from Not measured, or state that on OTP 28 the timing gap collapses. I ran it.
6. Recommendation sentence "The 2.1 µs check" should not be justified by a speed advantage over A.
