## Circularity review

- p2/p3 forgeries (Invoice with identical fields, float into int) are genuine and each route has a Direct* control. Not circular.
- The "term narrowed by naming the record" refusal is a real checker diagnostic (reproduced in my Nar module), not a harness filter.
- Patches do not special-case inputs; B is `{ok,_} when not Public`, A deletes the gate. Not circular.
- `output.txt` was not filtered; my diff is clean.
- Weak spots (not circular, but framing): (a) the "0 bytes, 0 ns for ints" bench is constructed so the guard is provably dead (finding 4); (b) the timing feeds one always-valid Order to a monomorphic site (disclosed in section 4 "not re-measured"); (c) "tuple element (int case)" only - the record-in-tuple route (`FromTuple((Cart,int))`) is equally real: caught by base, silent under B, silent under C. I confirmed it.

3. C's tag-on-record-field cost on a program that has such fields.
