## Circularity
Neither patch special-cases inputs: checker.patch adds two generic `comparison/1` clauses, parser.patch one `negate/2` clause. Both
are the minimal obvious fix, so the author's probes could pass under them by construction only in the sense that the probes test the
single feature the patch adds. They do catch a wrong patch for `>=`/`<` with a right-hand `-5`. They do NOT exercise: the
left-literal mirrored clause (`flip`) of A, nested negation, empty ranges, typing of a negative literal as an expression, or
division. The author's claim "probes fixed before patches" cannot be checked (no history); the probe set is too narrow rather than rigged.

`range(N,N)`. So B changes the type of every `-N` expression; A leaves it as `int`.

## Required corrections to the brief
1. Delete "Same acceptance set as A" / "Evidence here does not separate them on behaviour". Measured differences: (a) nested negation
   `-(-5)`, `- -5` accepted by B only; (b) a negative-range refinement can be returned from a literal (`G() -> -4`) under B only; A needs a
   `type_of(e_neg(e_int))` change as well to match; (c) B newly refuses `x / -0` (a correct refusal that A and baseline miss; baseline crashes at
   runtime). These favour B but make B wider than "1 line, same effect": it alters expression typing and the zero-divisor check, and the gates must be run for that.
