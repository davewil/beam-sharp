## New findings the brief omits
- **bsc beams already carry abstract_code**: `xref:add_directory` on bsc's R.beam gives `{ok,[{{'R','Client',0},{'Elixir.Req',new,1}}]}`. So "which foreign modules does this artefact need" is answerable today from the .beam with no compiler change. This undercuts the claim that the artefact "records nothing" for tooling purposes, and strengthens C/"do nothing".
- **`code:which(erlang)` -> `preloaded`** (an atom), the most common foreign module (20 uses). Option C's "derive app from path" (P5 filename logic) is undefined for it; C must special-case preloaded (and `cover_compiled`, escript-embedded modules).
