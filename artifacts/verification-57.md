## My independent probe (`real/t.escript`)

The brief's most load-bearing claim is that today `value >= -5` and its siblings are refused and that fix C (the g2 `negate/2` int fold) accepts them.

I compiled the **unmodified repo** `bs_types.erl` and `bs_check.erl` on OTP 25 with `+export_all`. It worked, contrary to the brief's "cannot be built here", which holds for `bsc` as a whole (the lexer needs leex from OTP 27). I fed `bs_check:refine(int, Pred, 1)` the real yecc ASTs from the `base_p`, `g2_p` and `g1c_p` parsers:

- base: `>= -5`, `>= -5 and <= 5`, `>= 1 or <= -1`, `-5 <= value`, `- -5`, `2 - 7`, `-x` all raise `{opaque_refinement,1}`. `<= 3 or >= 10` gives `int <= 3 | int >= 10`, and `!= 0` gives `int <= -1 | int >= 1`.
- g2: `>= -5` gives `int >= -5`. `>= -5 and <= 5` gives `-5..5`. `>= 1 or <= -1` gives `int <= -1 | int >= 1`. `-5 <= value` gives `int >= -5`. `- -5` gives `int >= 5`. `2 - 7` and `-x` are still refused.
- g1c: the same, except `- -5` is refused.

This **agrees with the model and the brief** cell for cell, and promotes the "refused today" and "rows 1-3 flip" claims from RECORDED/MODEL to measured against the real checker for the base, c-none and g2/g1c columns. It does not cover `c1/c2/c3`, which are proposals.

## Findings

1. **Label correction:** "RECORDED-NOT-RERUN / bsc cannot be built" understates what was possible. The real `bs_check:refine/3` is runnable on OTP 25, and now has been (above). No claim in the brief is contradicted.
2. **g1 is a hand-written strawman-ish variant.** It admits only `lident op int_lit`. It would also break atom and string refinements (`value == :ok`, F68 clauses at `bs_check.erl:5156-5164`) that the real `comparison/1` reads. The brief lists only `2 - 7`, `-x` and `WellFormed(value)` as g1 costs and does not mention this, so g1's cost is **under**stated. The recommendation (C, not A) is unaffected.
3. **Unflagged side effect of C (g2):** `type_of({e_int,_,N})` is the singleton `range(N,N)` (`bs_check.erl:2956`), whereas `type_of({e_neg,..})` is `int` (`:2963-2972`). Folding makes every `-5` expression a singleton `-5..-5` rather than `int`. This is probably beneficial (a `-5` argument would then satisfy a `Delta` parameter), but it alters inference and diagnostic text across the 1000+ tests. The brief only says "emission identical" and the pattern-legalisation side effect. Not measured by anyone.
4. Citation nit: the brief's "38b `:73-77`" is `wayfinder/prototypes/38b_divisor_expressiveness.sh:71-77`, a script whose `probe ... refused` lines are the recorded measurement (not a ticket file).
5. The ticket's own AST claim (`{e_op,'-',0,5}`) is stale as the brief says; verified (`yrl:587-591` comment plus F51 `e_neg`).
6. A negative-control weakness: the model's "REFUSED" for fixes that "refuse" `2 - 7` (g2, c1) is just the absence of a fold. That is real-checker-confirmed above for g2.

## Verdict

**Reproduces and holds.** All 10 re-run probes are byte-identical to the captured outputs. No probe was edited to manufacture a result; the yecc variants are exact diffs of the proposed fixes over an unmodified baseline, and conflict counts come from the unmodified grammar.
